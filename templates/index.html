Clinical Criteria & Rotterdam Consensus Guide

1. Dominant Follicle (DF) Condition

During the normal follicular phase, a single leading Graafian follicle expands beyond 10 mm in diameter (typically reaching 18–25 mm prior to ovulation). OvaScan AI recognizes dominant follicle expansion, preventing normal ovulating patients from receiving false-positive PCOS flags.

2. Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) Criteria

According to the international Rotterdam ESHRE/ASRM consensus, polycystic ovarian morphology is defined by ≥ 12 follicles measuring 2–9 mm per ovary, characteristically arranged in a peripheral subcapsular "string of pearls" ring around dense central stroma.

3. Normal Ovarian Architecture

Physiological resting ovaries exhibit balanced follicular reserves (< 12 antral follicles) without peripheral subcapsular clustering or pre-ovulatory dominant follicle expansion.
